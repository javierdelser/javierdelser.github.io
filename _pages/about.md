I am a **Principal Investigator (PI)** in the Applied Artificial Intelligence area of [TECNALIA Research & Innovation](https://www.tecnalia.com) and a **Distinguished Researcher** at the Department of Mathematics of the [University of the Basque Country (EHU)](https://www.ehu.eus). I am also a **Distinguished Visiting Professor** at the [University of Granada](https://www.ugr.es) (Spain) and a **Visiting Professor** at [BOKU University](https://boku.ac.at) (Vienna, Austria). I am also an external scientific member of the [Basque Center for Applied Mathematics (BCAM)](https://www.bcamath.org), and I am the Scientific Director of the [Joint Research Lab in Applied Artificial Intelligence (JRL-A2I)](https://www.jrl-a2i.science/), an initiative of TECNALIA, EHU and BCAM that builds a shared strategic research agenda around AI.

My research covers **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning** applied to modeling and optimization problems in industry, health, transportation, energy and mobility, among other sectors. I am best known for my work on **Trustworthy and Responsible AI**, including explainability, fairness, robustness, AI safety and human-centric AI, and on **Evolutionary Computation and Swarm Intelligence**. My main contributions in these areas include generative models for counterfactual explanations, frameworks for auditing and evaluating AI-based systems, applications of general-purpose AI systems, and evolutionary machine learning.

#### Highlights

- **Highly Cited Researcher** by Clarivate in 2025 and 2026 (in 2025, Computer Science, as one of two researchers in Spain in this category).
- Listed every year since 2021 among the **top 2% most influential researchers in AI** (Stanford University / Elsevier), in both the single-year and career-long rankings.
- **Member of the Scientific Panel of independent experts** advising the European Commission on implementing the **EU AI Act** (one of 60 members, 2026).
- Invited by the Science, Innovation and Universities Commission of the **Spanish Senate** to deliver an expert address on the economic and social impact of AI ([April 2025](https://www.senado.es/web/actividadparlamentaria/actualidad/video/index.html?s=15_S011022_020_01)).
- Member of the team that drafted the **Spanish R&D&I Strategy in Artificial Intelligence** (Government of Spain, 2019).
- **BRTA Prize for the Best Research Trajectory** (2023, out of more than 3,400 researchers), **Information Fusion Annual Best Paper Award** (2025), **IJCNN Best Paper Award** (2024) and **IEEE ITS Magazine Outstanding Research Paper Award** (2023).
- Member of [ELLIS](https://ellis.eu) (European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems), point of contact of TECNALIA for ELLIS, [CLAIRE](https://claire-ai.org) and [BDVA](https://www.bdva.eu), and IEEE Senior Member.
- Director of the **TECNALIA Chair in Artificial Intelligence** at the University of Granada; Distinguished Visiting Professor at the University of Granada and Visiting Professor at BOKU University (Vienna, Austria).

I earned my degree in Telecommunication Engineering at the University of the Basque Country (2003), a PhD in Control Engineering and Industrial Electronics (_Cum Laude_) at the University of Navarra (2006), and a second PhD in Information and Communication Technologies (_Cum Laude_, Extraordinary PhD Award) at the University of Alcalá (2013). I have authored more than 220 JCR-indexed journal articles and 230 conference contributions, supervised 20 doctoral theses (19 defended and one awaiting its defence), co-invented 9 patents, and taken part in more than 65 research projects and technology transfer contracts. I serve as Area Editor of _Information Fusion_ and Associate Editor of _Swarm and Evolutionary Computation_, _Cognitive Computation_ and _IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems_, and I was General Chair of IEEE ITSC 2023 (Bilbao).
