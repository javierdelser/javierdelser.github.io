Professional Summary

Researcher in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning, with a focus on Trustworthy and Responsible AI (explainability, fairness, robustness, AI safety, human-centric AI) and Evolutionary Computation / Swarm Intelligence. Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher (2025, 2026), listed since 2021 in the Stanford/Elsevier top-2% ranking in AI, member of the Scientific Panel advising the European Commission on the implementation of the EU AI Act (2026), author of 220+ JCR journal articles (85% Q1, 35% D1) and 230+ conference papers, 20 supervised PhD theses, 9 patents, and 65+ research projects and technology transfer contracts. Citations (Google Scholar, Oct 2026): 45,405; h-index 74; i10-index 271.
