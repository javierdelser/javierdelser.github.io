Transportation has been one of my main application domains for more than a decade. I was **General Chair of IEEE ITSC 2023** (Bilbao) and serve as Associate Editor of _IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems_.

<i class="fa-solid fa-route"></i> **Main contributions**

- Road traffic forecasting: a reference survey (IEEE ITS Magazine Outstanding Research Paper Award, 2023) {% cite lana2018road %} and a critical assessment of whether deep learning makes a difference {% cite manibardo2021deep %}.
- Deep learning for safe autonomous driving and vision-based scene understanding {% cite muhammad2021deep muhammad2022vision %}.
- Millimeter-wave vehicle-to-vehicle communications {% cite perfecto2017millimeter %}.
