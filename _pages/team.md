I have supervised **20 doctoral theses**, all defended with the highest grade (_Sobresaliente Cum Laude_). Two received the **Extraordinary PhD Award** and several earned the International Doctorate mention. I am currently supervising around 10 more at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), the University of Deusto, the University of Granada and other universities, several of them industrial doctorates co-funded by the Basque Government's BIKAINTEK programme. I have also supervised more than **45 BSc and MSc theses**.

Many of my former students now hold research and leadership positions at TECNALIA, at universities, and at companies such as IKUSI, INDRA and DYNICON.

> **Prospective students and postdocs.** If you are interested in trustworthy AI, AI safety, open-world learning, neuromorphic computing or evolutionary machine learning, feel free to contact me by email with your CV and a short statement of research interests. Positions are usually advertised through TECNALIA, UPV/EHU, the JRL-A2I and Marie Skłodowska-Curie networks such as IN-DEEP.
