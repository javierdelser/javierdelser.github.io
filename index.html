Javier Del Ser

Research Professor & AI Director at TECNALIA · Distinguished Researcher at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU) · Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher

TECNALIA, Derio (Bizkaia)

UPV/EHU, Dept. of Mathematics

BCAM, Bilbao

I am a Research Professor and AI Director at the Applied Artificial Intelligence area of TECNALIA Research & Innovation and a Distinguished Research Scientist at the Department of Mathematics of the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU). I am also an external scientific member of the Basque Center for Applied Mathematics (BCAM), and I coordinate the Joint Research Lab in Applied Artificial Intelligence (JRL-A2I), an initiative of TECNALIA, UPV/EHU and BCAM that builds a shared strategic research agenda around AI.

My research covers Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning applied to modeling and optimization problems in industry, health, transportation, energy and mobility, among other sectors. I am best known for my work on Trustworthy and Responsible AI, including explainability, fairness, robustness, AI safety and human-centric AI, and on Evolutionary Computation and Swarm Intelligence. My main contributions in these areas include generative models for counterfactual explanations, frameworks for auditing and evaluating AI-based systems, applications of general-purpose AI systems, and evolutionary machine learning.

Highlights

  • Highly Cited Researcher by Clarivate in 2025 (Computer Science; one of two researchers in Spain in this category), confirmed again for 2026.
  • Listed every year since 2021 among the top 2% most influential researchers in AI (Stanford University / Elsevier), in both the single-year and career-long rankings.
  • Member of the Scientific Panel of independent experts advising the European Commission on implementing the EU AI Act (one of 60 members, 2026).
  • Member of the team that drafted the Spanish R&D&I Strategy in Artificial Intelligence (Government of Spain, 2019).
  • BRTA Prize for the Best Research Trajectory (2023, out of more than 3,400 researchers), Information Fusion Annual Best Paper Award (2025), IJCNN Best Paper Award (2024) and IEEE ITS Magazine Outstanding Research Paper Award (2023).
  • Member of ELLIS (European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems), point of contact of TECNALIA for ELLIS, CLAIRE and BDVA, and IEEE Senior Member.
  • Director of the TECNALIA Chair in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Granada, where I am also a Distinguished Visiting Professor.

I earned my degree in Telecommunication Engineering at the University of the Basque Country (2003), a PhD in Control Engineering and Industrial Electronics (Cum Laude) at the University of Navarra (2006), and a second PhD in Information and Communication Technologies (Cum Laude, Extraordinary PhD Award) at the University of Alcalá (2013). I have authored more than 220 JCR-indexed journal articles and 230 conference contributions, supervised 20 doctoral theses, co-invented 9 patents, and taken part in more than 65 research projects and technology transfer contracts. I serve as Area Editor of Information Fusion and Associate Editor of Swarm and Evolutionary Computation, Cognitive Computation and IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, and I was General Chair of IEEE ITSC 2023 (Bilbao).
