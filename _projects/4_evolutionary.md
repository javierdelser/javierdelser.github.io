Since my second PhD (2013), **bio-inspired optimization and metaheuristics** have been a constant thread in my research, both as a field in its own right and as a tool for designing and improving learning systems (**evolutionary machine learning**).

**Main contributions**

- Critical reviews of where bio-inspired computation stands and where it should go {% cite del2019bio %}, comprehensive taxonomies of nature- and bio-inspired algorithms {% cite molina2020comprehensive %}, and a widely adopted tutorial on the design, experimentation and application of metaheuristics to real-world problems {% cite osaba2021tutorial %}.
- **jMetalPy**, an open-source Python framework for multi-objective optimization with metaheuristics {% cite benitez2019jmetalpy %}.
- Evolutionary **multitasking** and transfer optimization, including adaptive multifactorial optimization for multitask reinforcement learning {% cite martinez2021adaptive %}.
- Harmony search and its applications {% cite manjarres2013survey %}, and bio-inspired computation for transportation {% cite delser2020bioinspired %}.
