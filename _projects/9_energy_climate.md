Applied AI for the energy transition and for climate science: forecasting and anomaly detection in power and renewable energy systems, predictive maintenance of industrial and offshore assets, and machine learning for weather, climate extremes and complex systems.

<i class="fa-solid fa-bolt"></i> **Energy and industry**

- Robustness of power grids viewed as complex networks {% cite cuadra2015critical %} and modelling of electricity consumption during the COVID-19 pandemic {% cite khan2023modelling %}.
- Renewable energy assets: uncertainty-aware motion prediction of floating offshore wind turbines {% cite tamuly2026adaptive %}, anomaly detection in wind turbine operation {% cite abanda2024reconstruction %}, and degradation estimation in photovoltaic systems {% cite riano2026selfsupervised %}.
- Data fusion and machine learning for industrial prognosis in Industry 4.0 {% cite diez2019data %}.

<i class="fa-solid fa-cloud-sun-rain"></i> **Climate, weather and complex systems**

- Persistence in complex systems (_Physics Reports_) {% cite salcedosanz2022persistence %}.
- Machine learning for extreme atmospheric events: analysis, characterisation, prediction and attribution {% cite salcedosanz2023analysis %}.
- Long-term air temperature prediction {% cite fister2023accurate %} and estimation of ground-level air temperature in urban areas {% cite delgadoenales2025machine %}.
