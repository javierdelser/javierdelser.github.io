AI for Health, Industry, Energy & Climate

Medical imaging, industrial prognosis, power grids and climate extremes.

Applied AI research with partners in healthcare, manufacturing, energy and environmental science.

Health: brain tumor classification (Muhammad et al., 2020), fast MRI with Swin Transformers (Huang et al., 2022), airway segmentation with fuzzy attention (Nan et al., 2024), and data harmonisation for digital healthcare (Nan et al., 2022).

Industry and energy: data fusion and ML for industrial prognosis in Industry 4.0 (Diez-Olivan et al., 2019), and robustness of power grids viewed as complex networks (Cuadra et al., 2015).

Climate and complex systems: persistence in complex systems (Physics Reports) (Salcedo-Sanz et al., 2022) and ML for extreme atmospheric events (Salcedo‐Sanz et al., 2023).
