This line explores how **pretrained foundation models** (vision-language models and LLMs) can guide reinforcement learning agents in environments with sparse rewards, through intrinsic motivation, high-level language prompts and structured task decomposition.

**Main contributions**

- Intrinsic motivation with pretrained foundation models (NeurIPS 2024 workshop) {% cite andres2024fostering %}.
- Guiding RL agents with high-level language prompts (NeurIPS 2024 workshop) {% cite ruizgonzalez2024words %}.
- LLMs for structured task decomposition in RL problems with sparse rewards {% cite ruizgonzalez2025large %}.
