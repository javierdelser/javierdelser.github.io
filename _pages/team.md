## <i class="fa-solid fa-sitemap"></i> Research groups

### <a id="tecnalia-core-ai"></a><i class="fa-solid fa-microchip"></i> TECNALIA Core AI

The **[AI unit](https://scienceportal.tecnalia.com/p/en/organisations/ai)** is one of the cores of the **DIGITAL** division of [TECNALIA Research &amp; Innovation](https://www.tecnalia.com), the largest applied research and technology organisation in Spain and a member of the Basque Research and Technology Alliance (BRTA). It is TECNALIA's centre of expertise in artificial intelligence, spanning fundamental AI research and its transfer to industry, energy and environment, sustainable mobility, autonomous systems, health and business intelligence. I lead its scientific direction in AI as **Research Professor and AI Director**. I previously headed TECNALIA's Artificial Intelligence &amp; Big Data platform and the OPTIMA (Optimization, Modeling and Analytics) area.

### <a id="mathmode-upvehu"></a><i class="fa-solid fa-square-root-variable"></i> MATHMODE · UPV/EHU

The **Group on Applied Mathematical Modeling, Statistics, and Optimization ([MATHMODE](https://www.mathmode.science/))** is based in the Department of Mathematics of the Faculty of Science and Technology of the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU). It is recognised as a **Research Group of Excellence (A)** by the Basque Government and is funded as a consolidated research group (IT1294-19, IT1456-22 and IT1866-26). I have been a senior researcher of the group since 2019, working on its applied AI research line.

Its goals are to **develop knowledge** in applied mathematics, AI, statistics and scientific computing, to **transfer** this knowledge to industry and institutions for the benefit of society, and to **train new researchers** and attract and retain talent.

**Research areas**

<p>
<span class="chip">Applied numerical methods for PDEs</span>
<span class="chip">Neural-network-based numerical methods</span>
<span class="chip">Applied artificial intelligence</span>
<span class="chip">Deep learning for fast inversion</span>
<span class="chip">Applied statistics &amp; optimization</span>
<span class="chip">Advanced finite element methods</span>
<span class="chip">Scientific computing</span>
</p>

**Applications:** health, renewable energy and industry, including geophysics (simulation and inversion of borehole resistivity measurements for geosteering, elasto-acoustic waves in porous rocks) and structural health monitoring of bridges and offshore wind platforms.

**Team:** David Pardo (Ikerbasque Research Professor, group leader), Inmaculada Arostegui (co-leader), Javier Del Ser, Julen Alvarez-Aramberri, Judit Muñoz-Matute, Irantzu Barrio, Josu Doncel, Iker Malaina, Eli Alberdi, Carlos Gorria, Urtzi Ayesta, Josu Najera, Amaia Iparragirre, Imanol García, Ibai Laña, Izaskun Oregi, Magdalena Strugaru, Shima Baharlouei, Alejandro Duque, Xalbador Otxandorena, Leire Garmendia and Gontzal Sagastabeitia.

**Initiatives supported by MATHMODE:** the Joint Research Lab in Applied AI (JRL-A2I), the Talent Attraction Day in Applied Artificial Intelligence, and the Degree in Applied Artificial Intelligence and its Mathematical Foundation at UPV/EHU. Projects include the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Network IN-DEEP and the Basque ELKARTEK projects BEREZ-IA, EGOKIA, 3KIA and RUL-ET.

<p>The <a href="https://www.jrl-a2i.science/" target="_blank">JRL-A2I</a> is a joint initiative of <b>TECNALIA</b>, the <b>University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU)</b> and the <b>Basque Center for Applied Mathematics (BCAM)</b>. It brings together experts in computer science, engineering, mathematics and social sciences to close the gap between academic AI research and industry, and it aims to become an international reference in applied AI research and a hub for talent. I am its <b>Scientific Director</b> and a member of its Steering Committee, together with David Pardo, Elisabete Aramendi and Eneko Agirre.</p>

**Research lines:** physics-informed neural networks · natural language processing · multimodal data fusion · trustworthy AI, XAI, bias and ethics · open-set recognition and lifelong learning · computer vision · metaheuristic and Bayesian optimization · AutoML and meta-learning · uncertainty estimation · causal analysis · reinforcement learning · stream learning and concept drift · distributed AI (federated learning, edge AI).

## <i class="fa-solid fa-user-graduate"></i> Doctoral supervision

I have supervised **20 doctoral theses**, all defended with the highest grade (_Sobresaliente Cum Laude_). Two received the **Extraordinary PhD Award** and several earned the International Doctorate mention. I am currently supervising around 10 more at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), the University of Deusto, the University of Granada and other universities, several of them industrial doctorates co-funded by the Basque Government's BIKAINTEK programme. I have also supervised more than **45 BSc and MSc theses**.

Many of my former students now hold research and leadership positions at TECNALIA, at universities, and at companies such as IKUSI, INDRA and DYNICON.

> **Prospective students and postdocs.** If you are interested in trustworthy AI, AI safety, open-world learning, neuromorphic computing or evolutionary machine learning, feel free to contact me by email with your CV and a short statement of research interests. Positions are usually advertised through TECNALIA, UPV/EHU, the JRL-A2I and Marie Skłodowska-Curie networks such as IN-DEEP.
