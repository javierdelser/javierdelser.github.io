I have taught at the University of Navarra, Mondragon University, the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), the University of Málaga and Yachay University (Ecuador), and I have taught specialized AI courses for companies and public institutions in Spain and Latin America. In total I have about **4,000 hours** of teaching experience.
