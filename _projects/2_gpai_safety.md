Foundation models and other **General-Purpose AI Systems (GPAIS)** raise new scientific and societal questions. This line studies what makes a system general-purpose, how such systems can be evaluated, audited and governed, and which risks emerge when they are deployed at scale. I also serve on the Scientific Panel of independent experts that advises the European Commission on implementing the **EU AI Act**.

<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i> **Main contributions**

- A definition, taxonomy and responsible-governance framework for GPAIS {% cite triguero2024general %}, and a survey of how evolutionary computation can design and enrich them {% cite molina2025evolutionary %}.
- Analysis of **AI autophagy**, the degradation that follows when generative models are trained on AI-generated data (_Nature Machine Intelligence_) {% cite xing2025caveats %}.
- Using general-purpose AI as an **auditor** of responsible AI requirements {% cite delser2026from %}.
- Toxicity in large language models: definitions, datasets, detectors and detoxification {% cite villatecastillo2026systematic %}, together with privacy-leakage auditing through membership inference attacks {% cite jimenezlopez2025membership %}.
