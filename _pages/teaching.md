Teaching has run alongside my research since 2003. I have taught at the University of Navarra (TECNUN), Mondragon University, the University of the Basque Country (EHU) and the University of Málaga, given invited lectures at several Spanish universities, and taught specialised AI and data science courses for companies and public institutions in Spain and Latin America. In total I have about **4,000 hours** of teaching experience.
