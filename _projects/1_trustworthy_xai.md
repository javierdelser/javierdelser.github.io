How can people understand, trust and safely govern machine learning models? This research line builds the conceptual foundations of **Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)** and **Responsible AI**, and develops new methods for explaining models in ways that are actionable and reliable.

<i class="fa-solid fa-magnifying-glass-chart"></i> **Main contributions**

- One of the most cited references in the field: a taxonomy of XAI concepts, methods and challenges toward responsible AI {% cite arrieta2020explainable %}, followed by a 2023 review of what is known and what remains to be done {% cite ali2023explainable %} and the community manifesto of open challenges for _XAI 2.0_ (Information Fusion Best Paper Award 2025) {% cite longo2024explainable %}.
- A framework that connects AI principles, ethics and the seven requirements for trustworthy AI with auditable responsible-AI systems and regulation, including the EU AI Act {% cite diaz2023connecting %}.
- **Counterfactual explanations** that are plausible, minimal and adversarially robust, produced with generative models {% cite delser2024generating %}, and counterfactual and black-box explainers for object detectors and 3D point clouds {% cite andres2025dcrisp andres2024black holzinger2025enhancing %}.
- Information fusion as an enabler of robust, explainable and trustworthy medical AI {% cite holzinger2022information morenosanchez2025design %}.
