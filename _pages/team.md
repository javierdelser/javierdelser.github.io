## <i class="fa-solid fa-sitemap"></i> Research groups

### <a id="tecnalia-core-ai"></a><i class="fa-solid fa-microchip"></i> TECNALIA Core AI

The **[AI unit](https://scienceportal.tecnalia.com/p/en/organisations/ai)** is one of the cores of the **DIGITAL** division of [TECNALIA Research &amp; Innovation](https://www.tecnalia.com), the largest applied research and technology organisation in Spain and a member of the Basque Research and Technology Alliance (BRTA). It is TECNALIA's centre of expertise in artificial intelligence, spanning fundamental AI research and its transfer to industry, energy and environment, sustainable mobility, autonomous systems, health and business intelligence. I am a **Principal Investigator (PI)** of the unit. I previously headed TECNALIA's Artificial Intelligence &amp; Big Data platform and the OPTIMA (Optimization, Modeling and Analytics) area.

### <a id="mathmode-upvehu"></a><i class="fa-solid fa-square-root-variable"></i> MATHMODE · EHU

The **Group on Applied Mathematical Modeling, Statistics, and Optimization ([MATHMODE](https://www.mathmode.science/))** is based in the Department of Mathematics of the Faculty of Science and Technology of the University of the Basque Country (EHU). It is recognised as a **Research Group of Excellence (A)** by the Basque Government and is funded as a consolidated research group (IT1294-19, IT1456-22 and IT1866-26). I have been a senior researcher of the group since 2019, working on its applied AI research line.

Its goals are to **develop knowledge** in applied mathematics, AI, statistics and scientific computing, to **transfer** this knowledge to industry and institutions for the benefit of society, and to **train new researchers** and attract and retain talent.

## <i class="fa-solid fa-user-graduate"></i> Doctoral supervision

I have supervised **20 doctoral theses**: 19 have already been defended, all graded _Sobresaliente Cum Laude_, and the 20th (María Arostegi) has been deposited and is awaiting its defence date. Two of them received the **Extraordinary PhD Award**, and many earned the International Doctorate mention. I am currently supervising 11 more, mainly in the doctoral programmes of the University of the Basque Country (EHU), several of them industrial doctorates co-funded by the Basque Government's BIKAINTEK programme. Many of my former students now hold research and leadership positions at TECNALIA, at universities, and at companies such as IKUSI, INDRA and DYNICON.

> **Prospective students and postdocs.** If you are interested in trustworthy AI, AI safety, open-world learning, neuromorphic computing or evolutionary machine learning, feel free to contact me by email with your CV and a short statement of research interests. Positions are usually advertised through TECNALIA, EHU, the JRL-A2I and Marie Skłodowska-Curie networks such as IN-DEEP.

        "</ul>";
      L.polyline([[43.263, -2.935], [g.lat, g.lon]], { color: "#0b7bbf", weight: 1, opacity: 0.35, dashArray: "4 4" }).addTo(map);
      L.circleMarker([g.lat, g.lon], { radius: 6 + 2 * g.people.length, color: "#fff", weight: 1.5, fillColor: "#0b7bbf", fillOpacity: 0.85 })
        .addTo(map)
        .bindPopup(html)
        .bindTooltip(g.inst);
    });
